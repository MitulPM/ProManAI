# ProManAI — Product Manager Interviewer

An AI-powered **Product Manager Interviewer** that feels like a real PM interview.

The user should be able to **interact with the interviewer through voice**, answer questions naturally, think aloud, ask clarifying questions, and navigate a realistic interview experience.

The interviewer should respond dynamically based on the user's answers — asking follow-ups, challenging assumptions, introducing constraints, and adapting the conversation just like a real interviewer would.

The rest is for the team to figure out:

**What should the experience be? What types of interviews should it support? What should the interviewer evaluate? What should the UX look like? What is the MVP? What should be built?**

*Let the team plan, decide, design, and build it.*
